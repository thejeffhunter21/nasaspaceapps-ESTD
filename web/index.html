Are two regions really warming at different rates?

One region being significant and another not does not prove they differ. This runs the direct test: a seasonal Mann-Kendall test on the monthly difference between the two regions.

Data quality

Where the measurements are solid

NASA's quality control drops a cell's month when clouds, smoke or sensor issues make the reading unreliable. Those months stay missing here; nothing is filled in. Most cells have nearly the full 264 months; a few have more gaps, which makes their individual trends less certain.
